Scheduling with operational rules.

Availability, lead time and duplicate checks to organise fleet inspection scheduling.

The problem and my contribution

Rules before empty slots

A shared schedule must account for capacity, lead time and inspection frequency per vehicle. Without checks, coordination depends on manually reviewing each booking.

Automation in Google Sheets

I work with an inspection schedule and Apps Script to organise bookings according to operational rules. The focus is input validation and consistent scheduling criteria.

Rules and checks

Lead time and frequency

Check a minimum 48-hour lead time and avoid booking the same vehicle more than once in the defined week.

Availability and traceability

Scheduling must respect configured hours and capacity. Useful validation explains why a request needs review.

Try a rule

Simplified browser recreation: checks lead time, day, slot and duplicates. It uses a Monday-to-Sunday week, demonstration slots and the device’s local clock. It does not create real bookings.

Public demonstration · fictional data

Validate a request

The demo slots do not represent the company’s current capacity. Production code and integrations are not published.

Outcome and limitations

Observable outcome

A schedule with explicit rules and validation to organise work. The demonstration makes the approach inspectable without accessing the internal tool.

Measurement still needed

No time saving or reduction in missed appointments is claimed without measurement. Production also needs concurrency, permissions and capacity changes to be addressed.
