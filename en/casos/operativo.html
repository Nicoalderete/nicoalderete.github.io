Context and responsibility

Several operational fronts

Customers, collections, departures and operational coordination generate information with different cadences and needs. Management needs an organised view of what requires attention.

Supervision + reporting

In Customer Service at deRentas, I combine operational follow-up with metrics, reports and tracking tools. I connect day-to-day work with information useful to supervisors and management.

How I organise information

A defined cutoff

State the period, source and update date. Separate consolidated information from pending data so a prototype is not mistaken for a final close.

Defined metrics

Document the population, calculation and reference. Keep permanent departures separate from temporary suspensions, and case volume separate from resolution time.

A view for each audience

The executive summary highlights signals and decisions. Operational detail retains the context needed to review causes, exceptions and records.

Action follow-up

Connect deviations with next steps, owners and progress reviews. A metric is useful when it helps structure a management discussion.
