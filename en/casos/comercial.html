Sales, revenue and business context.

Two academic projects in Power BI and Tableau exploring products, cities and periods, with documented scope.

Power BIDAXTableauExcel

Two tools, two implementations

Coderhouse academic projects on electronics sales in 2019. They are grouped by topic for readability; their sources, filters and totals are not assumed to be equivalent.

The business question

How do volume, revenue and contribution vary by product, city and period? Separating these metrics prevents a single ranking from hiding important differences.

Scope of the work

Data preparation and visual exploration for academic exercises. The dashboards do not establish business deployment or a measured increase in sales.

Power BI

Excel preparation, relationships between orders, products, categories and locations, a date table and DAX measures for exploring commercial performance.

View case and files ↗

Tableau

Period, product and city views, with rankings and matrices to distinguish sales volume from monetary values.

View case and files ↗

Documented quality and limitations

Power BI

The public review documents missing-value treatment and inconsistencies in transcribed formulas. The PBIX was not recalculated in that review; report figures remain reported results.

Tableau

Available evidence includes screenshots. The workbook and filters were not verified in the documented review. NULL values and a cost ranking require reconciliation.

Contribution based on included costs is not presented as net profit. Totals from the two projects need reconciliation before comparison.
